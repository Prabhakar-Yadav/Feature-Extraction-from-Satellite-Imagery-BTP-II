# SegFormer-B2 Zero-Shot Transfer Benchmark Report

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Evaluated Architecture:** `RoadEnhancedSegFormer` (MiT-B2 Transformer Backbone + High-Resolution scSE Edge Refinement Stem)
- **Evaluated Checkpoint:** `best_segformer.pth` (Trained on Cartosat-1 2.5m GSD)
- **Test Datasets:** 3 New Indian Metropolitan Scenes from Cartosat-2E (0.60m GSD, 4-Band Multispectral):
  1. **Pune City** (`2673727101`): Historical organic core, commercial boulevards, Mula-Mutha river, rail terminal
  2. **Pune Hinjwadi** (`267372781`): Planned IT corridor, high-rise tech parks, Mumbai-Pune Expressway flyovers
  3. **Chennai** (`267372791`): Coastal metropolis, avenue grids, broad-gauge rail corridors, Buckingham canal
- **Scale Matching Strategy:** Physical scale preservation via $2,133 \times 2,133$ window downsampling (`cv2.INTER_AREA`) to $512 \times 512$ ($2.50\text{m}$ GSD = $1,280\text{m} \times 1,280\text{m}$ ground coverage).
- **Total Benchmark Coverage:** 30 Strategic Patches ($49.15\text{ km}^2$ total urban ground area).

## 4. Key Takeaways & Technical Insights
1. **Strong Corridor Connectivity in Modern Road Grids:** SegFormer achieved up to **70.0% Road IoU (10m SpaceNet)** on wide arterial highways (e.g. `pune_hinjwadi_10_toll`: 70.0%, `pune_hinjwadi_05_wakad`: 69.3%, `pune_city_01_core`: 68.8%, `pune_hinjwadi_04_expressway`: 62.4%). The MiT-B2 attention mechanism successfully links elongated asphalt segments.
2. **Zero-Shot Transfer Generalization:** Despite never being trained on Pune or Chennai, SegFormer reliably transferred features across diverse urban layouts without retraining.
3. **Impact of Panchromatic Monochromatic Conversion:** The model was fed synthetic panchromatic images ($Y = 0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B$) rather than native multispectral bands. While roads and built-up structures transferred strongly, distinguishing barren soil from low vegetation strictly on gray levels remains naturally ambiguous compared to using native NIR multispectral bands.
